# Limitations of target and mechanism identification

- The cache verifies operational targets and export alignment, not latent biological EZ truth. SOZ, composite status/resection cues and spreadsheet clinical designations are different proxies.
- Historical adapter source versions and complete cache-creation command flags are not embedded. Current source hashes, stored source categories and exact run-union crosschecks support lineage, but cannot provide a perfect execution-time source attestation. Original outcome/quality-based cohort inclusion cannot be fully reconstructed from this export alone.
- Independent HUP SOZ/resection fields are missing; target-definition disagreement cannot be quantified where fields are absent. Missing is not zero.
- The 22 LZU first-record/index differences are fully explained by EZ union across cached runs. They are not evidence of wrong clinical annotation. Labels were not changed.
- Small source subcategories are pooled at center level (minimum five public unique patients). No rare-source patient or channel records, values, paths, thresholds or coefficients are released.
- Source categories are strongly confounded with center and composition. Descriptive error differences do not establish a causal label-noise ceiling or justify relabeling by surgical outcomes.
- Threshold oracle and true-count TopK use target labels and cannot be deployed. Threshold headroom is not a unique causal decomposition of classification error.
- B8 support labels are retrospective simulated clinical targets, not prospectively available clinician input. A single label budget/policy is not a clinical active-learning study.
- PCA4 is a constrained probe. Failure cannot show the complete 88D representation lacks usable patient information; half-support AP improvement is counterevidence to that broad claim.
- FIT-OOF teachers use smaller memberships, independently FIT-only preprocessors and ten-pass MC means; VAL uses deterministic final A0. This legal cross-fitting provenance does not guarantee score-distribution transfer. No oracle or in-sample workaround was introduced.
- All development patients have been repeatedly used in the broader project. Bootstrap intervals screen evidence but are not independent confirmation; there was no new outer TEST evaluation.
