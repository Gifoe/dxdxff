# Historical evidence and exact-duplication admission

72 available local and remote-tracking Git references were searched for PURN,
UniqueFeatureNorm, unique LayerNorm/RMS and RMSNorm. No exact previously trained
A0 unique56-statistic RMS experiment was found. Relevant current checkouts,
original source-free constructor/trainer/preprocessor/selection, feature group
audit and all 88 manifest entries were inspected. Proceed under the frozen
four-arm study, not a claim of universal novelty.

The two geometry/bottleneck directories exist on their named available branches
rather than this checkout. Their actual reports and source extraction/replay/
view code were read via git, without switching or executing their data loaders.
D2, no-Z, PMA, LN-deletion, TabM and PR-Spline reports/code were read locally.
This distinguishes inspection of published historical aggregates from reopening
private outer performance or letting historical outcomes alter the fixed task.

Ordinary RMS normalization is prior mathematical practice. PURN uses the same
RMS principle but an immutable 56-coordinate statistic set and the original
88D affine/downstream interface. It is not feature deduplication: duplicate and
zero positions remain in the Linear88 input. It is not LN deletion: normalization
and all176 affine parameters remain. Its patient-relative aspect is inherited
from unchanged Patient Z, not a patient-specific fitted affine or new predictor.

The all88 versus unique56, centered versus uncentered 2x2 experiment is the
specified hypothesis test. Statistical distortion alone cannot prove that the
old LayerNorm harms localization; both component controls must be beaten.
At development admission, no old outer score, label, checkpoint or scientific
prediction file is loaded: B0 development checkpoints and FIT/VAL replay alone
are authoritative inputs. Only after that study failed did the user separately
authorize outer completion. That extension reuses exact frozen B0 outer scores
without changing them; no model or threshold is selected using those results.
