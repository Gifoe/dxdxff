**Terminal: `DIRECT_THRESHOLD_VALIDATION_GATE_FAILED; RANK_DISTILLATION_WORTHWHILE=NO`.** The experiment is complete without A1 outer-test access. It used one historical seed and previously viewed Task-1 outer outcomes only for the separate Part B retrospective diagnostic. It does not establish a multiseed or fresh-confirmation result.

## Part A — what cross-fitting changed

Historical B0 replayed exactly: patient Macro-F1 `0.616167` on its archived outer test; five original checkpoints matched channel scores to `≤1.48e-7`. The new meta-training stage generated 25/25 patient-level cross-fit predictions inside outer-fit, with no predicting model trained on its held-out patient. Across folds, OOF B0 logits had an average standard-deviation increase of `0.0924` relative to B0 predictions on its own fit patients. This is a material distribution difference, but smaller inner-training cohorts are a confounder; it does not by itself prove that in-sample exposure caused the prior CalibRank failure.

The direct linear, score-only threshold learner A1 did **not** transfer. Mean five-fold outer-validation patient Macro-F1 was `0.591867` versus `0.628380` for frozen B0, a **−3.651 pp** difference with `0/5` positive folds. Mean EZ-F1 declined `4.875 pp`. The same A1 model's fit-OOF Macro-F1 was `0.632921` (optimistic in-sample evaluation of the meta learner), revealing another train-to-validation gap. Its mean distance to each validation patient's retrospectively optimal threshold interval (`0.710` logits) exceeded the B0 global anchor's distance (`0.575`). The validation oracle ceiling itself remained `0.702841`, about `7.45 pp` above B0; A1 recovered **none** of this gap and instead degraded. Prior CalibRank B1 had validation Macro-F1 `0.632194` under its own validation-selected threshold, whereas A1 here uses a fixed 0.5 final decision. Those procedures are not a clean head-to-head comparison, but there is no evidence that direct threshold prediction improved on the failed prevalence route.

The locked outer-test gate required ≥`+1 pp` validation gain, ≥`3/5` positive folds and no mean EZ-F1 drop beyond `2 pp`. It failed on all three conditions. **A1 outer-test arrays were not read, and no A1 outer result is reported.** Optional PCA hidden context A2 was omitted before training because separately trained cross-fit B0 hidden axes are unaligned to each other and to the final historical B0; a pooled PCA would not be a defensible matched descriptor. Whether a properly aligned low-dimensional hidden context helps remains unanswered.

## Part B — ranking ceiling, not a model result

Archived B0/BCR ledgers align exactly on 80 patients, five folds, 7,635 channel identities and labels. Percentile-rank interpolation over the fixed 11-point grid gave B0's patient-threshold oracle `0.702602`; the **retrospectively selected global** lambda `0.8` reached `0.713157` (+`1.056 pp`). Ranking improved (EZ-AUPRC `0.531996→0.559563`, MRR `0.733031→0.779238`), but the oracle ceiling remained below both locked thresholds (`0.72` and `+1.5 pp`). Allowing **patient-specific, label-selected** lambdas reached `0.739260`; that stronger number is wholly non-deployable. Within the hardest 10–30% of B0 channels, BCR/mix AUPRC tended to improve, but MRR and Top-1 often worsened, so the benefit is not uniformly concentrated at the decision boundary. `RANK_DISTILLATION_WORTHWHILE = NO` under the predeclared rule. No distillation student was trained.

## Decision

The next step is **neither** this direct patient-threshold learner nor BCR-to-B0 distillation as currently specified. The direct-threshold hypothesis has substantial retrospective oracle headroom but no label-blind validation transfer; the ranking mix improves ordering but not enough to satisfy its ceiling gate. Do not claim deployable Macro-F1 near 0.70, SOTA, or a superiority result from these oracles. Any future attempt requires a new, separately locked mechanism and validation design—not retuning this run after seeing its outcomes.
