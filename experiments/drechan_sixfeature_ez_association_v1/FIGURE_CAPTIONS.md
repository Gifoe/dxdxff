# Figure captions

## Figure A. Physiological Differences Between EZ and NEZ Channels

Signed rank-biserial effects comparing EZ with NEZ channels were calculated
separately within each of 80 patients for the six fixed physiological features,
each represented by the original across-seizure mean and population SD summaries.
Dots/squares show equal-patient mean effects; horizontal lines indicate nominal
95% percentile confidence intervals from 10,000 patient-bootstrap draws (seed42).
The dashed line denotes zero association. Positive effects indicate higher
**baseline-subtracted summary values** in EZ channels; features were not
sign-flipped. Blue circles and orange squares denote mean and SD coordinates,
respectively. BH q-values correct 12 two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests.
Zero-effect patients remain in means and bootstrap intervals but are excluded
from signed-rank tests. The seven single-seizure patients have zero SD-coordinate
effects and remain included. The summaries average all valid pre/post windows
of each seizure record after same-channel pre-onset subtraction, not only ictal
0–20 s. Features were historically selected on this previously viewed cohort;
the analysis is exploratory association, not independent external validation.

## Figure S1. Source-specific physiological associations

Mean within-patient signed rank-biserial effects and nominal 95% patient-bootstrap
intervals are shown separately for HUP (36 patients), LZU (21), multicenter (15)
and pediatric (8), retaining the original source labels and all patients.
The marker coding and summary definitions match Figure A. Source intervals are
descriptive, not multiplicity-adjusted source-wise significance tests. Positive
overall effects do not imply consistent source or individual-patient directions;
pediatric RMS mean and partial high-gamma SD have negative point estimates and
intervals including zero. These labels are sources, not verified single hospitals.

## Figure S2. Within-patient effect distributions

Fixed 0.05-wide histograms show all 80 patient effects for every one of the 12
coordinates. Blue indicates across-seizure means and orange across-seizure SDs.
The dashed line is zero. Bins span [−1, 1]; the last bin includes +1.
Zero-effect patients are retained. Only aggregate bin counts, not identifiable
patient rows or exact individual effects, were exported from the trusted server.
