Conclusion: Qualified temporal record descriptors do not establish a stable
conditional increment over original physiology and Raw context in this screen.

Archetype: quantitative grid. Python/matplotlib only, following the established
project workflow and saved backend. No reused private template or artificial data.

Macro-F1 after choosing each k's subset on CHOICE, assessed on HELD. Lines use
the registered appearance-equal mean; shading is sample SD across five original
FIT folds, not a confidence interval or five independent cohorts. No k is
chosen using these curves.

paths in each context. These paths overlap in training patients. Empty sets
are permitted. Panel d compares new-only physiology with original7, and the
three incremental contexts with their own no-new-feature baseline. All four
contexts' AUROC and Macro-F1 differences are shown with
unadjusted10000 paired patient-ID cluster percentile intervals. It does not
display a confirmatory test or a full M8 result.
