## What the new evidence changes

The earlier statement that the six historical record operators lacked an
admissible measurement has been superseded by source/qualification evidence.
Their distinct Raw250 implementations are now described physiologically and
actually tested predictively. This resolves an evidence gap without changing
the outcome of either failed predictive continuation gate.

Ten new coordinates meet the association rule, but only three of eight
applicable coordinates meet directed repeatability. Strong marginal effects
therefore do not justify a new model, and positive source directions with
three/four pediatric patients do not establish precise pediatric transport.

No combined38-group/76-coordinate model or subset search has been executed.
The source-qualified library and the searched candidate spaces must remain
distinct. A future joint experiment would need a substantive new rationale
and a separate prospective design; it cannot be presented as already done or
automatically launched to rescue failed6-family results. Independent
generalization still needs data that were not repeatedly used in development.

## Current decision

Do not relabel original7 as globally optimal, adopt a post hoc union or reopen
Outer. Keep original models and all negative results. Use the atlas to explain
which quantities are available, which differences recur, and why these facts
alone do not identify a stable conditional minimum. The full scientific goal
remains unproven; no goal-complete claim is made here.
