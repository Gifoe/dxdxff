## Next useful evidence, without changing v2

The legally observed Development results already expose an unresolved design
issue: physiology-only linear cardinality preference does not necessarily
transfer to the neural families. Moreover, selecting a feature without
conditioning on Raw is not the same question as whether it complements Raw.
A separately registered **internal-training-only** Raw-conditional screen is
therefore a meaningful follow-up. Its encoder must exclude every assessment
patient; fullFIT-trained encoders may not be used as if inner cross-fitted.
The zero-feature candidate must be eligible to win. No v2 protocol, model,
threshold or sealed Outer score is modified, and no new Outer access is part of
this follow-up. Positive discovery remains unproven and is not guaranteed.

## Executed fixed-score sensitivity results

The mean Jaccard of the five final, possibly different-cardinality fold sets
is0.25. This is observed overlap, not an independently sampled stability
estimate. The10 pair rows are published without treating pairs as independent
replicates.

For selected Concat minus Raw-only0–20, ordinary unadjusted ranking changes
remain AUROC+0.006007 and AP+0.007880. Under a six-primary-endpoint Bonferroni
percentile sensitivity calculation, their intervals are respectively
[−0.001902,0.013947] and[−0.000983,0.016762]. All six endpoint intervals include
zero. Under the eight-endpoint calculation they remain inconclusive. This
additional calculation was specified after the original outcomes were known:
it is a transparent sensitivity diagnostic, not a new prospective confirmatory
claim or a change to any original estimate, threshold or chosen model.

Thus the actual evidence is weaker than "a small positive mean exists" would
suggest. It does not establish stable multi-endpoint Raw increment or justify
calling the broad scientific objective achieved.
